Outreach

"Más allá del eclipse: entender el cielo nocturno"

As part of my outreach activities, I taught the public course “Más allá del eclipse: Entender el cielo nocturno” at UNED Zamora in 2026. Over fourteen hours of online and in‑person sessions, the course offered an accessible introduction to astronomy: from humanity’s historical interpretations of the sky to modern scientific understanding of the Earth, the Solar System, stars, galaxies, and the large‑scale structure of the Universe. Participants learned how to navigate the night sky, identify constellations, understand the basic operation of telescopes, and explore the physical processes that shape cosmic evolution.

The full course description can be found on the following website UNED-COURSE, and the recorded lectures are available for viewing on a separate platform VIDEO LECTURES.

Observations

In addition to this course, I regularly collaborate with amateur astronomers and local astronomy groups, participating in public observing sessions with small telescopes and sharing practical guidance for exploring the night sky.
